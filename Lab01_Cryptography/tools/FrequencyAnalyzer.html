Frequency Analysis

Why does this need a lot of text? Frequency analysis relies on the Law of Large Numbers. A short message (like a ~26-character personalized SIP string) can easily have one letter spike to 20-30% of the total just by chance -- that's noise, not a real signal. Only once you have a few hundred letters does the count settle down toward English's real-world distribution (E ~12.7%, T ~9.1%, A ~8.2%, ...). Want to see a clean example first?
