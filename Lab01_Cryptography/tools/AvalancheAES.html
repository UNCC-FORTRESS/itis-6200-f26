Bonus: The "ECB Penguin" Effect

AES-ECB encrypts every 16-byte block independently with the same key, so identical plaintext blocks always produce identical ciphertext blocks. On text that's invisible, but on an image it means large flat-colored regions stay visually recognizable after "encryption" -- the classic demo is encrypting a simple logo/line-art image and still being able to tell what it is.

Upload any image below. It's first shrunk to a tiny low-detail thumbnail, then blown back up with nearest-neighbor scaling (no smoothing) -- this guarantees large blocks of identical pixels, which is what actually makes the ECB pattern visible. Simple logos/line art with flat colors show the effect best; a detailed photo will look noisier even after this trick.
