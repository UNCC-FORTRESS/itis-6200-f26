# Missing Screenshot Penalty Policy

This policy applies to every lab (Lab 00-08) and supersedes any older, softer "deduct a couple points per missing screenshot" language you may see in historical grading notes. It exists because every lab's grading model assumes a screenshot is how you *prove* you did the work — a correct written answer with no screenshot to back it up is unverifiable, not just incomplete.

**The rule:** if a submission is missing one or more required full-desktop screenshots (see `guidelines.txt` for what counts as a valid screenshot — full desktop/taskbar or menu bar visible, not a cropped window), the maximum score achievable on that assignment is capped as follows:

| Missing screenshots | Maximum achievable score |
|---|---|
| 1 | 60% of total points |
| 2 | 50% of total points |
| 3 | 40% of total points |
| 4 | 30% of total points |
| 5 | 20% of total points |
| 6 | 10% of total points |
| 7 or more | 0% |

**How it works:** count every required screenshot missing from the submission (across all parts/steps of the lab). Grade the submission normally first — written analysis, correctness, everything — to get a raw score. Then, if any screenshots are missing, cap that raw score at the percentage in the table above. The cap applies **regardless of how strong the written analysis is** — a submission with perfect written answers but two missing screenshots is capped at 50%, full stop.

A screenshot that's present but doesn't meet the formatting requirements (not full-desktop, missing required markup) is graded under the normal rubric's screenshot-quality criteria, not this policy — this policy is specifically for screenshots that are **absent entirely**.

This is intentionally strict. Screenshots are the only verifiable evidence that the personalized (SIP-derived) experiment was actually run, which is the whole point of the personalization scheme — see `guidelines.txt`'s Academic Integrity section.
